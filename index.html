Starting to trade is confusing, and loud.

A beginner meets margin, ticks, strikes, stops, a thousand strategies and a thousand people selling certainty. Most rooms sell hope and alerts. Very few show a real record, and nobody shows what does not work.

Too much to learn

Three very different markets, each with its own language, risks and costs. Beginners give up, or learn the expensive way.

Too many confident voices

Alert rooms and influencers rarely publish an audited record. The beginner cannot tell skill from luck.

No one says what fails

Every strategy video shows the winning trades. The losing ideas, the costs and the slippage are left out.

Seven pillars

Each pillar exists in part already. KAT has been tested in the open since September 2026.

01 · ASK KAT

An honest answer to any question

"Do 30-minute candles push against the trend before they close?" "Is a CPI move a sure bet?" "Should I widen my stops?" Typed in plain language, answered from 15 years of data: how often, after costs, and why.

02 · EXPLAINED

Why did the engine do that?

Every engine decision shown live: what it saw, which rule allowed or blocked the trade, and what happened next. Beginners learn discipline by watching it work.

03 · LEARN

Paths for stocks, options, futures

Short lessons from "what is a tick" to testing a first strategy. Every lesson ends with a task in the simulator.

04 · PRACTISE

Simulators with a coach

Real contracts and prices, realistic fills and stops. After each practice trade the coach reviews your stop, your size, your plan and what came next.

05 · PROTECT

Training wheels

The risk rules KAT learned the hard way: a daily loss limit, one trade at a time at the open, a profit lock that switches to careful mode, a cost check before every trade.

06 · WHAT FAILS

The library of failed ideas

Every failed test as a two-minute lesson with its data: chasing CPI, adding to winners, wider stops, trend-day rules. Nobody else teaches this.

07 · EARNED

A public track record

Every engine's forward results after costs, with its failures. Signals appear only for an engine that has passed its 12-month test, with its full record beside every call, and only if they demonstrably help people.

DAILY

A companion every day

A plain-language morning brief before the open, the engine thinking aloud through the first hour, an evening review of the day in charts, and a weekly live session with a real trader.

TOGETHER

Small cohorts

Learners start together, share simulator trades and compete on discipline, not profit.

Learning from the best-known trading room

The Traveling Trader has about 25,000 members, about 300,000 YouTube subscribers and a community running since 2017. People clearly value what he gives them. We study it closely, keep what works, and add what is missing.
